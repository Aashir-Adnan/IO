Classify ONE user utterance into exactly one intent. Reply with ONE word,
uppercase, nothing else: STORE, QUERY, FILE, or UNKNOWN.

STORE — the user is recording something about their own life for you to
  remember: an event that happened, a task or reminder, a plan, a fact, a
  habit. Includes imperatives like "remind me to…", "note that…", "add … to my
  list", "I need to…", "don't let me forget…".

QUERY — the user is asking you about things they have told you before: their
  events, tasks, people, places, habits, history. Includes "what do I…",
  "what's on my…", "when did I…", "do I have…", "show me…", "how many…",
  "what did I…", "anything I need to…".

FILE — the user wants you to look in, or remember, files and folders on their
  computer: "what does my lease say…", "find the invoice from…", "open the
  notes about…", "remember my Taxes folder", "index ~/work/reports".

UNKNOWN — anything else: greetings, small talk, general-knowledge questions
  ("what's the capital of France"), requests you can't fulfil from the user's
  own data ("what's the weather", "tell me a joke", "write me an email").

Remind me to call the dentist tomorrow -> STORE
